# Mapping Mayonnaise Packs, Operators and Gatekeepers

*Complement to the earlier Desk Research "O Foodservice brasileiro e a maionese nas cozinhas profissionais" (Kraft Heinz / Quero, "Inside the Kitchen" ethnography: 18 operators, 10 in São Paulo and 8 in Recife). Version of 04/11/2026. It deepens three areas of the earlier report (packaging, operator classification and stakeholders) and connects them. It does not replace the earlier report. Where a topic was already covered there, this document points to the chapter instead of repeating it.*

---

**Bottom line.** Mayonnaise reaches Brazilian kitchens in **eight packaging families**, but the names the trade uses for them do not line up with the objects. The word "sachê" covers everything from a 7 g portion sachet to a 2.8 kg flexible bulk bag. The Unilever Food Solutions catalogue calls its 1.6 kg pack a "saco", its own product pages call the 2.8 kg pack a "bag", and Vigor adds a 1 kg "manga" whose physical structure no source describes. "Doypack" itself is a trademark of Thimonnier for the stand-up pouch, not a generic term. Classification of operators is just as layered. The only official taxonomy, IBGE's CNAE Division 56 (*Alimentação*), sorts businesses by where and for whom food is served. It has no code for hamburguerias, pizzerias, dark kitchens or food trucks, and it places the Brazilian bakery-café (padaria) outside foodservice altogether. Size, chain ownership, service channel and kitchen model come from other systems (LC 123/2006, Galunion, ABF, Crest/IFB, ABERC, municipal law) that coexist on the same business rather than excluding one another. The stakeholder layer adds one more distinction: the **sales channel** (the route the product travels), the **commercial model** (how the intermediary sells: delivery, self-service, sales reps, app) and the **company type** (the legal or business identity) are different things. Tenda Atacado and Grupo Mateus each run three or four commercial models at once, and the cash-and-carry chains reject the label "atacarejo" for themselves. Taken together, the new notes show that who decides on brand and pack depends more on ownership and operating model than on the cuisine label. A franchisor's approved list fixes both brand and pack. In the 65% of bars and restaurants registered as individual micro-entrepreneurs (MEI), one owner does the choosing, buying, cooking and food-safety duties. Between the two ends, public evidence is thin, and most cells in the maps below are hypotheses for the 18 field visits. Almost all new evidence in this round comes from search-result snippets, because page access was blocked. Several corrections to the earlier report follow, and the most consequential is that the ABF "78% use approved suppliers" figure could not be re-confirmed.

# PART 1 — PACKAGING: MAPPING AND NOMENCLATURE

## Eight packaging families, four separate descriptors

### 1.1 What is actually sold in Brazil

**[FACT]** Snippet-level evidence supports **eight format families** of mayonnaise packaging in Brazil (all *(S)*, sources in §1.3 and §1.4). The **rigid pail (balde)** has only one size found, 3 kg. **Flexible bulk packs** of 1.0–3 kg are called "saco", "bag", "pacote", "sachê", "pouch", "refil" or "manga", depending on the manufacturer and the seller. **Retail flexible pouches** of 180–700 g are almost always called "sachê" by retail. The **single-serve portion sachet (sachê de porção / sachê individual)** weighs 7–8 g and is sold in cases of 144–192. The **squeeze bottle (squeeze / bisnaga)** runs from 170 g to 930 g–1 kg. The **plastic or PET jar (pote / PET)** runs from 200 g to 1 kg. The **mini squeeze portion (mini-bisnaga)** of 30 g is sold in packs of 50. The **glass jar (pote de vidro)** is historical in Brazil: Hellmann's arrived in 1962 and launched a 500 g glass jar in 1964, and no current Brazilian glass SKU was found ([Mauá – monografia, resumo (PDF)](https://maua.br/files/monografias/resumo-historia-embalagem-maionese-brasil-210921.pdf)).

**[FACT]** Equally important is what was **not** found for mayonnaise in Brazil: gallon jug (galão), bag-in-box, sealed portion cup (PC cup / copinho selado), a bottle sold as "top-down" and pump-dispenser systems. All of these appear only as international references [INT]. "Top Down" is a product name in **Portugal** (Hellmann's 238 g and 412 g; Heinz Top Down), while Brazilian retail says "squeeze" ([Continente – Hellmann's Top Down](https://www.continente.pt/produto/maionese-top-down-hellmanns-6885792.html); [Continente – Heinz Top Down](https://www.continente.pt/produto/maionese-top-down-heinz-5558745.html)). Bag-in-box sauce dispensing exists in Europe ([ESM Magazine – Maustaja](https://www.esmmagazine.com/packaging-design/introducing-the-maustaja-bag-in-box-air-free-dispensing-concept-27521); [Oliehoorn](https://www.oliehoorn.nl/en/assortiment-professionals/sausdispenser-bag-in-box/)), as does the Italian 4 kg professional "busta" ([ABC Foodservice IT – Biffi](https://www.abcfoodservice.it/en/mayonnaise/maionese-biffi-gastronomica-busta-kg4)). The pump systems (Heinz Keystone, Sauce-O-Mat, Hellmann's Premium Dispenser) are in Chapter 6.2 of the earlier report. Searches for portion cups returned only empty disposable 50 ml sauce pots with lids for delivery ([Mercado Livre](https://lista.mercadolivre.com.br/pote-descartavel-tampa-50ml)), which kitchens fill themselves. **[ANALYSIS]** If a kitchen shows a glass jar or a sealed portion cup of mayonnaise, it is most likely a reused container, an import or a house-portioned pot, not a Brazilian SKU.

### 1.2 Format, structure, material and closure are four different questions

The earlier report warned that "sachê" is ambiguous (§6.1). The new notes show that the problem is wider. Brazilian trade names mix up four descriptors that packaging technologists keep separate. The table below sets out the distinction as an **analytical synthesis**, using real terminology only.

| Descriptor | Question it answers | Real terms (EN / PT-BR) | Evidence for mayonnaise in Brazil |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Format** | What does it look like and how is it handled? | pail / balde; jar or tub / pote; squeeze bottle / squeeze, bisnaga; stand-up pouch / "doypack", sachê, pouch; flat bulk bag / saco, bag, pacote; portion sachet / sachê individual | Commercial names from UFS, Tenda, Magalu and others *(S)* |
| **Structure** | How is it built? | rigid injection-moulded; rigid blow-moulded (bottle); flexible laminate; **stand-up pouch** (bottom gusset, stands on shelf); **pillow pouch** (single web, top and bottom seals plus a back seal); **spouted pouch**; stick pack; flow wrap | Pillow pouch is the typical structure of single-serve sachets ([Unibo packaging morphology](https://adu.unibo.it/pack/it/morfologie/pillow-pouch)); "flow wrap" is used for single-serve mayonnaise packets in a US trade glossary ([PMMI ProSource](https://www.prosource.org/glossary)) [INT]. **No Brazilian mayonnaise bulk pack has a verified structure** |
| **Material** | What is it made of? | PP (resin code 5), HDPE / PEAD (2), PET (1), multilayer laminates ("7 / Outros"), PE//PE mono-material | Historical "embalagem flexível em PET com bico dosador" for Hellmann's in 1998 and a 1 kg stand-up pouch in the Hellmann's timeline ([Mauá](https://maua.br/files/monografias/resumo-historia-embalagem-maionese-brasil-210921.pdf)); "PET" in Hellmann's/Arisco 500 g names. Generic empty buckets cite "polipropileno virgem grau alimentício", but they are not mayonnaise SKUs ([Magalu "balde lacre"](https://www.magazineluiza.com.br/busca/balde+lacre)) |
| **Closure / dispensing** | How is it opened, dosed and reclosed? | lid with tamper seal (tampa com lacre); screw cap (tampa rosqueável); flip-top valve; spout with cap (bico dosador com tampa); tear notch (picote); zipper (zíper); pump (bomba) | "Com bico dosador" confirmed only for a Hellmann's 1 kg listing and for Fugini 180/300 g retail pouches (2023). Nothing else verified |

**The "doypack" question.** **[FACT]** Early stand-up pouch work was done in France by Léon and Louis Doyen. Doyen was president of Thimonnier, which registered "Doypack" as a trademark. Sources disagree on the date (1962 in mirror articles, 1963 in a Festo/Thimonnier profile) ([Wikipedia – Stand-up pouch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stand-up_pouch); [Festo – "Standing strong"](https://www.festo.com/us/en/e/blog/in-practice/standing-strong-id_28442)). A Mauá monograph covers the history of the stand-up pouch in Brazil ([Mauá – stand pouch (PDF)](https://maua.br/files/monografias/completo-historia-embalagem_-stand-pouch-210909.pdf), not read). Brazilian trade press uses "stand up com bico dosador" for spouted stand-up pouches, for example in a Predilecta jam launch ([Mercado & Consumo, 2019](https://www.mercadoeconsumo.com.br/2019/12/12/predilecta-lanca-geleias-em-embalagem-stand-up-com-bico-dosador/)). **[ANALYSIS]** "Doypack" therefore names a **structure** (and a trademark), "pouch" names a **format family**, and "sachê", "bag" or "refil" are **commercial names** that say nothing about structure. In this document, "stand-up pouch (doypack)" is used only when the gusseted, standing structure is meant, and "spouted" is added only where a spout is confirmed. The UFS "saco" of 1.6–2.8 kg is called a "flexible bulk bag (structure to confirm)" until the pack is seen.

**Brand vocabulary.** **[FACT]** Each manufacturer has its own words *(S)*. Unilever Food Solutions uses "Balde", "Saco", "Bag", "Sachês" and "Squeeze" ([UFS 2022](https://www.unileverfoodsolutions.com.br/dam/global-ufs/mcos/sla/brazil/mstb/Catalogo%202022.pdf); [UFS 2025-2](https://www.unileverfoodsolutions.com.br/dam/global-ufs/mcos/sla/brazil/mstb/Catalogo-2025-2.pdf)). Vigor uses "Bag", "Manga", "Pote" and "Pouch" in its relaunch ([Gironews](https://gironews.com/informacoes-de-fornecedores/vigor-reformula-linha-de-maioneses-com-foco-em-cremosidade-e-sustentabilidade/)), while Tenda sells the same line as "Sachê" and "Pacote". # PART 2 — CLASSIFICATION OF FOODSERVICE OPERATORS

## One official taxonomy, many commercial lenses

The earlier report sized the segments (§1.3–1.5, §3.1) and proposed a seven-group working typology, (a) to (g) (§1.4). ### 2.1 Existing classification systems and their status

**Official: CNAE 2.3, Division 56.** **[FACT]** The only official taxonomy of operators is IBGE/CONCLA's National Classification of Economic Activities (*Classificação Nacional de Atividades Econômicas*, CNAE 2.3), Section I "Accommodation and food" (*Alojamento e alimentação*), Division 56 "Food" (*Alimentação*). Its logic is **where and for whom food is served**: consumption on the premises (class 56.11), ambulant service (56.12), and food prepared for companies, events, private canteens or home consumption (56.20). It does not classify by cuisine, brand, chain or channel. **[FACT] Padarias sit outside Division 56.** A bakery is coded either as industry, **1091-1/02** (bakery and confectionery products with predominantly own production, Section C), or as retail, **4721-1/02** (bakery and confectionery with predominantly resale, Section G). **Official: size (porte).** **[FACT]** Legal size is a separate axis set by Complementary Law 123/2006 (*Lei Complementar 123*): microenterprise (ME) up to R$ 360 thousand of annual gross revenue, small enterprise (EPP) above that and up to R$ 4.8 million ([LC 123/2006 – Senado](https://legis.senado.leg.br/norma/572878/publicacao/16418577)). The individual micro-entrepreneur (MEI) may earn up to R$ 81 thousand a year, may have no partner and may hire at most one employee. 2026 accounting sources still cite R$ 81 thousand, and bills to raise the cap appear only as proposals ([Jornal Contábil](https://jornalcontabil.com.br/noticia/conheca-as-diferencas-entre-microempresa-pequena-empresa-e-mei/); [FPA – PLP 108/2021](https://fpagropecuaria.org.br/?p=35830)). The current ceiling must be confirmed on the Portal do Empreendedor. The MEI regime is also a list of **permitted occupations**, each mapped to a CNAE subclass (CGSN Resolution 140/2018, Annex XI). Snippets map "proprietário(a) de lanchonete independente" to 5611-2/03, "proprietário(a) de bar e congêneres, sem entretenimento, independente" to 5611-2/04, the marmiteiro, salgadeiro and doceiro independentes to 5620-1/04, and the pipoqueiro and ambulant sellers to 5612-1/00 ([Contabilidade.com – marmiteiro](https://contabilidade.com/blog/cnae-5620104-marmiteiro-a-independente-pode-ser-mei-quanto-paga-e-quando-desenquadrar-do-mei/); [VRI Consulting – MEI table](https://vriconsulting.com.br/cnaes/atividades-permitidas-microempreendedor-individual-mei.php?pagina=13); [Prefeitura SP – ocupações MEI](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/desenvolvimento/mei/ocupacoes)). **Conflict:** one summary claimed 5611-2/03 is not MEI-eligible, and one page title mapped "lanchonete independente" to 5611-2/04. The majority reading is lanchonete MEI = 5611-2/03. Sebrae adds a **statistical** size scale by employees for commerce and services (micro up to 9, small 10–49, medium 50–99, large 100+), which it describes as a methodological proxy for CAGED data ([Sebrae – Relatório CAGED (PDF)](https://sebrae.com.br/Sebrae/Portal%20Sebrae/Anexos/Relatorio%20do%20CAGED%20-%2007%202018.pdf)).

The household budget survey (POF) classifies **items consumed** away from home, not establishments (earlier report §1). Two regulatory categories act as de facto classifications. São Paulo's **Municipal Law 17,853/2022** regulates "dark kitchens" as complexes of industrial kitchens with no on-site consumption, each kitchen at least 12 m² and complexes at least 300 m apart, regulated by Decree 62,365/2023 ([Câmara Municipal SP](https://www.saopaulo.sp.leg.br/blog/e-lei-funcionamento-das-dark-kitchens-e-regulamentado-na-cidade-de-sao-paulo/); [Machado Meyer](https://www.machadomeyer.com.br/pt/inteligencia-juridica/publicacoes-ij/imobiliario/regras-de-funcionamento-das-dark-kitchens-em-sao-paulo)). This is a municipal zoning category, not a CNAE. ### 2.3 Overlap: what excludes and what coexists

**[FACT]** Within one system, categories are meant to be exclusive. A CNPJ has one main CNAE, but it can add secondary ones (for example 5620-1/04 main + 5620-1/03 secondary) ([CNPJá](https://cnpja.com/office/32626892000127)). Official exclusion notes make some codes mutually exclusive: 5611-2/03 excludes ambulant service (5612-1/00), 5611-2/01 excludes event buffet (5620-1/02), 5620-1/04 excludes 5611-2/01, 5620-1/02 and 5620-1/03, and 4721-1/02 excludes 1091-1/02 ([SES-MG (PDF)](https://www.saude.mg.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/11-10-2024-Atividades-Nivel-de-Risco-I-sujeito-ao-controle-sanitario-1-291.pdf); [BuscadorNCM](https://buscadorncm.com.br/cnae/5620104)). Size changes over an operator's life: an MEI selling snacks often exceeds the R$ 81 thousand ceiling quickly ([Contabilidade.com](https://contabilidade.com/blog/cnae-5611203-lanchonetes-casas-de-cha-de-sucos-e-similares-pode-ser-mei-quanto-paga-e-quando-desenquadrar-do-mei/)).

**[ANALYSIS]** Across systems, almost everything coexists. **Mutually exclusive by design:** one main CNAE per CNPJ; one LC 123 size at a time (MEI vs. ME vs. EPP); within a contract, self-managed vs. concession; one Crest channel per transaction. **Coexisting by nature:** service channels (dining room + counter + delivery + take-away); ownership forms within one brand (own stores + franchises); cuisines in multi-brand kitchens; commercial and institutional activity in the same contractor. **Ambiguous or boundary cases:** the padaria (industry or retail in CNAE, foodservice for Crest); delivery-only (5611-2/01 or /03 vs. 5620-1/04); the food truck (5612-1/00 vs. 5611-2/03 when parked at a fixed point; single truck vs. fleet); cafeteria and açaí shop (5611-2/03 vs. ABF "Comercialização"); prepared food in convenience stores and supermarkets (counted by Crest, not by CNAE 56).

# PART 3 — STAKEHOLDERS: THE MAYONNAISE CATEGORY ECOSYSTEM

## Channel, commercial model and company type are three different things

The earlier report drew a first value-chain diagram (§2.1), described each link (§2.2), tabulated who buys, decides, uses and consumes (§2.3) and outlined four go-to-market routes (§4.1). This part refines the chain with the new notes and adds role-level detail inside the establishment.

### 3.1 Commercial chain stakeholders

**The three-way distinction.** **[ANALYSIS]** The **sales channel** is the route the product travels to the kitchen (for example, manufacturer → distributor → operator). The **commercial model** is how the intermediary sells and serves: with delivery and a sales force, as self-service cash-and-carry, by telesales or WhatsApp, through an app, or under a contract with approved suppliers. The **company type** is the legal or business identity (wholesaler, distributor, retailer, platform, logistics operator). One company often runs several models at once. **[FACT]** Tenda Atacado (Guarulhos, SP) operates physical stores, **telesales for business customers (televendas para comerciantes)**, a **distributor arm with more than 200 sales representatives (atacado distribuidor)** and e-commerce ([Grandes Nomes da Propaganda](https://grandesnomesdapropaganda.com.br/anunciantes/tenda-atacado-lanca-campanha-ta-no-tenda-ta-na-mao/), V2). Grupo Mateus runs retail, cash-and-carry (Mix Mateus, now Novo Atacarejo) and **distribution wholesale (atacado de distribuição)**. Its B2B division had revenue of R$ 2.1 billion in 3Q25 (+26.8%), served more than 48 thousand customers a month and, when entering a new city, first uses the wholesale model (Armazém Mateus), with almost 2,000 sales representatives per a sell-side report of uncertain date ([BP Money](https://bpmoney.com.br/negocios/grupo-mateus-fecha-acordo-com-concorrente-e-avanca-no-nordeste); [XP](https://conteudos.xpi.com.br/acoes/relatorios/grupo-mateus-gmat3-uma-joia-garimpada-no-nordeste-iniciamos-com-recomendacao-de-compra/), V2). Atacadão (Carrefour) combines cash-and-carry stores with a B2B base. Note that ri.tenda.com belongs to Construtora Tenda, a homebuilder, not to Tenda Atacado.

**Wholesale (atacado) is not cash-and-carry (atacarejo).** **[FACT]** The Brazilian Association of Wholesalers and Distributors (ABAD) classifies the indirect channel by commercial model: distributor with delivery (distribuidor), general wholesaler with delivery (atacado generalista com entrega), self-service wholesaler (atacado de autosserviço) and counter wholesaler (atacado de balcão). The 2025 shares are in the earlier report (§2.2). A 2015 breakdown shows distributor 43.3%, wholesaler with delivery 31.9% and self-service 19.3% ([Bandamazonas, ABAD data](https://bandamazonas.com.br/?p=23778), V2). No official ABAD glossary definition could be read. A search summary attributes to ABAD the criterion that an "atacarejo" sells more than 50% to individual consumers (pessoas físicas), but the originating article was not identified (V3; candidates: [IstoÉ Dinheiro](https://www.istoedinheiro.com.br/questao-tributaria-divide-atacadistas-e-atacarejos/); [Exame](https://exame.com/economia/apos-conflito-atacarejos-se-unem-e-criam-nova-associacao/)). The self-service wholesale chains founded their own association, **ABAAS**, after a conflict inside ABAD, one trigger being ABAD's push to make the buyer's CPF mandatory on cash-and-carry invoices. A founder said: "We are cash & carry or self-service wholesale. 'Atacarejo' does not define us" ([Exame](https://exame.com/economia/apos-conflito-atacarejos-se-unem-e-criam-nova-associacao/); [Giro News](https://gironews.com/atacado-cash-carry/cash-carry-cria-associacao-28738/), V1). **[ANALYSIS]** In this document, "atacarejo" is used as the popular name of the format, while "cash-and-carry (atacado de autosserviço)" is the trade's own term. Neither is the same as a distributor or a wholesaler with delivery.

**Distributor economics.** **[FACT]** In a Brazilian distribution or commercial concession contract, the distributor buys and resells, earning the spread between purchase and resale price, not a commission ([Law Insider – contract template (PT)](https://lawinsider.com/pt/contracts/fMPzySL7TAt), V3). Territorial exclusivity conditional on purchase targets is described only in generic, non-Brazilian sources (V3). Manufacturers' complaints in an ABAD context cite "speculation, unfair margins and exclusivity policies" in negotiations with distribution wholesalers ([Giro News – ABAD president](https://gironews.com/eventos-especiais/presidente-da-abad-fala-sobre-nova-perspectiva-para-o-atacado-21389/), V2).

**Manufacturers and professional brands.** **[FACT]** The professional-brand layer now has confirmed owners (see the corrections table). **Kraft Heinz** (Quero, Heinz, Hemmer) described an indirect route through "partner distributors and specialized wholesalers" plus long-term partnerships with large players. In 2021 it helped partner distributors go digital to reach more points of sale, and it sold Heinz flavoured mini-sachets for foodservice nationally from Apr/2022 "through partner distributors and wholesalers" ([Giro News – solução conecta setor a PDVs](https://gironews.com/informacoes-de-fornecedores/solucao-conecta-setor-a-pdvs-64434/); [ABC da Comunicação](https://www.abcdacomunicacao.com.br/heinz-lanca-mini-saches-saborizados-para-o-food-service-brasileiro/), V2). **Unilever Food Solutions** claims Hellmann's is "the most remembered brand in Food Service Brasil" (Ipeso 2021, company claim) ([UFS catalogue (PDF)](https://www.unileverfoodsolutions.com.br/dam/global-ufs/mcos/sla/brazil/mstb/Catalogo-Produtos-Unilever-Food-Solutions.pdf)). **Kerry** (Junior) sells sandwich and salad sauces, seasonings, sweeteners and disposables, and announced Maionese Verde and Sweet Chilli for Burger Expo 2026 ([Giro News – Junior](https://gironews.com/food-service/junior-destaca-duas-opcoes-de-molhos-para-a-diferenciacao-de-cardapios/)). **Vigor (Grupo Lala)** had McDonald's, Starbucks and Applebee's among foodservice clients in 2017; whether mayonnaise was involved is not stated ([DairyReporter](https://www.dairyreporter.com/Article/2017/10/30/Grupo-Lala-completes-Vigor-Alimentos-acquisition/)). **Cargill** has a foodservice division with Mariana and Liza. **Seara (JBS)** sells Soya in a 3 kg bag; no new evidence this round. **Predilecta's** manufacturer was not verified. The earlier report's §5.2 remains the reference for the Unilever and Kraft Heinz retail shares.

**Specialized foodservice distributors and regional distributors.** **[FACT]** Few SP names could be confirmed. **Bidfood Brasil** (Bidcorp) acquired **Vinhais**, described as "one of the largest and most traditional distributors of the foodservice market in the state of São Paulo", strong in dairy, on 30/09/2021, and launched a credit initiative for operators, "Bidfood Bank" (V3 for the credit detail) ([Global Scope Partners](https://globalscopepartners.com/all-transactions/bidfood-brasil-and-vinhais/), V2). **MegaG Alimentos** has served foodservice since 2005 (location and range unverified) ([InfoJobs](https://www.infojobs.com.br/mega-g-alimentos), V3). **Monte Carlo Alimentos** (Cidade Dutra, SP) self-describes as a foodservice distributor ([Best Food Importers](https://bestfoodimporters.com/company/monte-carlo-alimentos/), V3). Delly's Food Service (SC) remains the largest foodservice-labelled name in the ABAD top 10 (earlier report). **For Pernambuco, no specialized foodservice distributor could be verified this round.** Directory-level results show only Distcarnes (meat, Recife) and AP Bebidas (beverages, Caruaru, entered Recife in 2021 with 2,500+ clients) ([Movimento Econômico](https://movimentoeconomico.com.br/economia/negocios/2021/11/16/com-sede-em-caruaru-ap-bebidas-inicia-operacao-no-recife/), V2). Cadan stays at the earlier V3 level. **[HYPOTHESIS]** In Recife, the specialized-distributor role is partly filled by retail groups' distribution wholesale (Armazém Mateus) and by local distributors whose names only operators can give.

**Cash-and-carry, membership clubs and supermarkets.** **[FACT]** Atacadão's 1Q25 sales grew 7.3% to R$ 20.7 billion, mainly on higher B2B volumes, as customers "tend to stock up in inflationary periods". In 3Q24, a slight slowdown in B2B consumption was attributed to deflation in July–August ([SuperVarejo](https://supervarejo.com.br/varejo/carrefour-brasil-registra-crescimento-de-36-nas-vendas-consolidadas-no-1t25); [Economic News Brasil](https://economicnewsbrasil.com.br/2024/10/23/carrefour-brasil-registra-vendas-de-r-295-bi-no-3o-trimestre-de-2024/), V1). Genial analysts expected B2B stockpiling in 2Q24 to weaken Assaí's 3Q purchases ([Genial](https://analisa.genialinvestimentos.com.br/acoes/assai/assai-asai3-previa-3t24-faltou-o-molho/), V2). Assaí is rolling out **Assaí Pay** card machines for B2B and B2C to the end of 2026, with lower take-up so far among B2B customers ([Monte Bravo (PDF)](https://www.montebravo.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Monte-Bravo-Analisa-Resultado-Assai-4T25.pdf), V2). **Roldão** operates only in São Paulo state with about 40 stores (Nov/2025) and digital sales of about 5–6% ([Giro News](https://gironews.com/atacado-cash-carry/em-2025-roldao-atacadista-preve-aumentar-sua-presenca-em-sp/), V2). **Spani** had 42 stores in SP and RJ in 2024 ([Mercado & Consumo](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/24/06/2024/noticias-varejo/spani-atacadista-foca-em-expansao-no-estado-de-sao-paulo-e-avanca-na-regiao-de-campinas/), V2). **Sam's Club**, a membership club (clube de compras) owned by Carrefour since 2022, had 51 stores at end-2023 and opened its first Northeast unit in João Pessoa in Jun/2024 as a "combo" with Atacadão ([Movimento Econômico](https://movimentoeconomico.com.br/economia/varejo/2024/06/20/joao-pessoa-ganha-combo-sams-club-atacadao-o-1o-do-nordeste/), V1). Its Recife presence was not verified. Supermarkets serve emergency purchases (earlier report §4.3).

**Sales representatives (RCA).** **[FACT]** The independent commercial representative (representante comercial autônomo, RCA) is the main selling arm of the indirect channel (earlier report §2.2). Scale evidence is new: Tenda's distributor arm has 200+ reps, Armazém Mateus about 2,000 (V2). Manufacturer sales forces sell through distributors: the UFS "Chef Gerador de Demanda" visits clients with the distributor's salesperson (earlier report §2.2). **[ANALYSIS]** The RCA sells and takes orders on commission. It does not buy or hold stock. Its catalogue or app is the first filter of which brands and packs a small operator is offered.

**Logistics operators and franchisor supply structures.** **[FACT]** Arcos Dorados' 20-F filings describe the McDonald's model: suppliers deliver to distribution centres that provide transport, warehousing and inventory planning for the restaurants. Suppliers are largely approved under McDonald's master franchise agreements, with written pricing agreements. Some DCs were formerly owned through **Axionlog**, split off on 16/03/2011, which provides distribution under a master commercial agreement ([SEC – 20-F FY2023](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1508478/000162828024019077/arco-20231231.htm); [20-F FY2018](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1508478/000095010319005187/dp105200_20f.htm), V1, not read in full). Brazil-specific coverage names **Martin-Brower** as the McDonald's logistics operator, in Brazil since 1982, with six DCs and more than 30 thousand deliveries a month in 2026 ([Mundo Logística](https://mundologistica.com.br/noticias/martin-brower-logistica-mcdonalds-kfc-cinemark), V2; the KFC and Cinemark relationships are inferred from the URL only). In 2010 its **Recife** DC ran 90 routes a month, about 10% of national distribution ([Conexão Tocantins](https://conexaoto.com.br/2010/09/13/mcdonald-s-inaugura-primeiro-restaurante-em-palmas), V2, old). **Conflict:** the 20-F names Axionlog, while Brazilian coverage names Martin-Brower; both are shown, neither is chosen. The Arcos Dorados Brazil fact sheet reports 84% local suppliers ([Fact Sheet 2Q22 (PDF)](https://www.arcosdorados.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/Fact-Sheet-2Q-2022-BRASIL.pdf)). **Zamp** (BK, Popeyes, Starbucks, Subway) administers **Subway's franchises and suppliers** in Brazil and focused Popeyes' expansion where it already had BK, "to maintain operational and logistics synergy" ([Meio & Mensagem](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/marketing/burger-king-starbucks-e-subway-zamp-expande-operacoes-no-brasil), V2). No named Zamp logistics operator was found. **Madero** runs a central kitchen (Cozinha Central) that produces "sauces, pasta and desserts" and, per a Mar/2026 report, supplies the whole network ([Mercado & Consumo, 2018](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/06/07/2018/noticias/grupo-madero-investe-em-ampliacao-de-sua-fabrica/); [Compre Rural](https://comprerural.com/nova-fabrica-da-madero-no-parana-tem-tamanho-equivalente-a-18-campos-de-futebol/)). Whether its mayonnaise is made centrally is not confirmed. Bob's has about 370 franchisees (2019) and a "central warehouse" with its own fleet in an undated company timeline ([Mercado & Consumo, 2019](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/21/03/2019/foodservice/controladora-do-bobs-adquire-cinco-pontos-comerciais-em-sao-paulo/)).

**B2B digital platforms and marketplaces.** **[FACT]** Frubana closed in Brazil (see corrections). **Cayena** connects suppliers to restaurants **without own logistics**, lists more than 30 thousand references, and restaurants buy **5–7 times a month**. "R$ 100 million" appears both as a funding round and as accumulated GMV, so sources conflict ([Brazil Journal](https://braziljournal.com/cayena-levanta-r-100-mi-para-ajudar-os-restaurantes-a-comprar-melhor/); [El Referente](https://elreferente.es/inversiones/cayena-marketplace-brasileno-levanta-175m-serie-a/), V1 with conflict). **Foozi** is a purchasing-intelligence platform that aims to cut cost of goods, with R$ 2 million raised in 2025 ([Monitor Mercantil](https://monitormercantil.com.br/startup-foozi-estima-gerar-r-1-bilhao-de-economia-para-o-setor-de-food-service/), V2). **BEES** (Ambev) carried 400+ items from about 40 manufacturers in 2022, plus BRF and GPA, and served about 800 thousand establishments in 2021 ([Mercado & Consumo – GPA no BEES](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/2022/06/23/grupo-pao-de-acucar-passa-a-vender-produtos-proprios-no-bees-plataforma-b2b-da-ambev/), V1). Whether mayonnaise brands are on BEES was not verified. **iFood Shop** sold more than 10 thousand items only to establishments with an active iFood registration ([Saipos](https://saipos.com/ifood/ifood-shop), V2); its 2026 status is unconfirmed. Mercado Livre carries foodservice mayonnaise through reseller-distributor stores (V3), but no dedicated restaurant B2B programme exists.

**Central purchasing.** **[FACT]** No Abrasel purchasing centre or restaurant purchasing cooperative could be verified. Abrasel's supplier-facing tools found are an equipment classifieds platform and training ([Giro News – Mercado Abrasel](https://gironews.com/food-service/anuncios-no-mercado-abrasel-60823/), V2). **[ANALYSIS]** Centralised buying in Brazilian foodservice happens mainly inside chains and franchisors (approval and central negotiation, earlier report §2.2) and in contract caterers. Intermediaries also finance operators (Bidfood Bank, Assaí Pay), which makes credit a lever of channel choice.

### 3.2 Roles inside the establishment

**Official occupational definitions.** **[FACT]** The Brazilian Classification of Occupations (*Classificação Brasileira de Ocupações*, CBO 2002) has distinct codes, read only through third-party mirrors (snippet). **2711-05 Chefe de cozinha** (synonyms include chef, executive chef, kitchen supervisor and encarregado de cozinha) "heads the kitchen, defines the menu, prepares recipe cards (fichas técnicas)… controls stock of inputs" ([Salário.com.br CBO 271105](https://www.salario.com.br/ocupacao/cargos/cbo-271105-cargos/); [BuscadorNCM](https://buscadorncm.com.br/cbo/271105)). **5132-05 Cozinheiro geral** includes restaurant cook and school cook (merendeiro). **5135-05 Auxiliar nos serviços de alimentação** covers kitchen assistant and helper (an HR forum suggests 5134-25 instead, a weaker source). **5134-35 Atendente de lanchonete** lists among its synonyms **"chapista de lanchonete"**, the snack-bar griddle cook, and includes preparing hot and cold snacks under good practices ([Salário 513435](https://www.salario.com.br/ocupacao/cargos/cbo-513435-cargos/)). **"Chapeiro" itself is not a confirmed CBO title.** Job boards sometimes file chapeiro ads under metalwork code 724415, a classification pitfall ([SINE João Pessoa (PDF)](https://joaopessoa.pb.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/vagas-periodo-30-a-03-de-janeiro.pdf); [BNE](https://www.bne.com.br/vaga-de-emprego-na-area-metal-mecanico-em-fortaleza-ce/chapeiro/4535271)). **5136-05 Churrasqueiro** has its own code. **1415-10 Gerente de restaurante** "plans, supervises, controls and evaluates services… to meet targets set by management", and **1415-15** is the bar and snack-bar manager ([Salário 141510](https://www.salario.com.br/ocupacao/cargos/cbo-141510-cargos/)). No CBO code was checked for restaurant buyer, stock keeper or sandwich assembler (montador).

**Regulatory roles.** **[FACT]** Under ANVISA's RDC 216/2004, as summarised in training material, every food service must have a person **responsible for food-handling activities** (responsável pelas atividades de manipulação), who "may be the owner or a designated, duly trained employee" and who supervises handlers ([IFCE EaD – Aula 07 (PDF)](https://capacitacao.ead.ifce.edu.br/pluginfile.php/18088/mod_resource/content/1/Aula%2007-%20Documenta%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20e%20Fun%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20do%20Respons%C3%A1vel%20pelo%20Setor.pdf), secondary). A Santa Maria (RS) decree requires that person to train the team at least once a year ([Câmara Santa Maria (PDF)](https://camara-sm.rs.gov.br/Content/upload/oldsite/importacao/anexos/DE_051_2011___CURSO_BOAS_PRATICAS_ALIMENTACAO.pdf), municipal). A **nutritionist as technical lead (responsável técnico)** is a Federal Council of Nutritionists (CFN) requirement for collective-feeding units and CRN-registered food companies (Resolutions 600/2018, 702/2021, 795/2024), not for ordinary snack bars ([CFN Res 702/2021](https://cfn.org.br/wp-content/uploads/resolucoes/resolucoes_old/Res_702_2021.html)). Resolution 600/2018 is under revision, and a 2026 CFN note says its minimum-staffing annex would lack a legal basis ([CFN – Res 857/2026 e PNAE](https://cfn.org.br/sistema-cfn-crn-e-fnde-discutem-impactos-da-resolucao-cfn-no-857-2026-no-pnae/)).

**What job ads say roles do.** **[FACT]** Chapeiro ads in hamburguerias list cooking "on the chapa following the house recipe standard", assembling sandwiches "following recipe cards and presentation standards", preparing ingredients for assembly ("cut vegetables, **assemble the sauces**") and, in one ad, "**prepare mayonnaise** and other items"; one example salary is R$ 2,100 plus benefits (Atibaia) ([Infojobs – chapeiro Campinas](https://www.infojobs.com.br/vaga-de-chapeiro-hamburgueria-campinas-em-sao-paulo__11728247.aspx); [Solutudo – chapeiro SJC](https://www.solutudo.com.br/empregos/sp/s-jose-campos/chapeiro-2830432); [BNE – Atibaia](https://www.bne.com.br/vagas-de-emprego-para-chapeiro-em-atibaia-sp); attribution of each phrase to each ad uncertain). Kitchen assistant ads ask for receiving and checking goods "by sensory criteria and expiry date", producing "sauces, sides, fillings and other assembly items", **recording the expiry date of opened or handled products** and storing by **first-expired, first-out (PVPS, primeiro que vence, primeiro que sai)** ([Vagas.com](https://www.vagas.com.br/vagas/v2825409/auxiliar-de-cozinha); [Massa Empregos](https://massa.com.br/emprego/auxiliar-de-cozinha/)). Kitchen-lead ads (a steakhouse in Jaguariúna; a Habib's group unit in Itapecerica) list input checks, storage, expiry control, waste monitoring and production planning; one generic ad adds "prepare shopping lists and follow up suppliers", recipe cards, yield and portions ([Solutudo – líder de cozinha](https://www.solutudo.com.br/empregos/sp/jaguariuna/lider-de-cozinha-2844103); [Infojobs – Habib's](https://www.infojobs.com.br/vaga-de-chefe-cozinha-itapecerica-em-sao-paulo__11709086.aspx); [Jooble](https://br.jooble.org/jdp/-3086625830915079714)). A stock-and-purchasing lead in SP manages suppliers "to reduce impact on cost of goods" ([Infojobs](https://www.infojobs.com.br/vaga-de-lider-estoque-compras-em-sao-paulo__11792474.aspx)). Buyer ads mention "development and approval of suppliers, quoting, negotiation and issuing orders", quoting at least two suppliers and negotiating price and terms ([BNE – comprador Salvador](https://www.bne.com.br/vagas-de-emprego-para-comprador-em-salvador-ba)). A chain's quality role combines **supplier approval** with restaurant audits and documentation ([Jooble – analista qualidade](https://br.jooble.org/jdp/1402907170941338296); [Agrobase](https://www.agrobase.com.br/oportunidades/2026/01/analista-pleno-de-qualidade-e-gestao-de-fornecedores-sao-paulo-sp/)).

**Role accumulation.** **[FACT]** An FGV EESP study with Abrasel (Dec/2024) counted **1,379,420 active bar and restaurant establishments** in Aug/2024, **65% MEI** and **94% microenterprises** (95% in another citation), and PNAD Contínua shows **4.94 million workers** in 2023 ([FGV EESP (PDF)](https://eesp.fgv.br/sites/default/files/files-eesp/bares_e_restaurantes_no_brasil.pdf); [Mercado & Consumo, 03/12/2024](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/03/12/2024/foodservice/estudo-mostra-impacto-do-setor-de-bares-e-restaurantes-para-a-economia-do-pais/)). Sebrae-PR cites "almost 5 million jobs" with about 40% informality ([Sebrae PR](https://sebraepr.com.br/comunidade/artigo/uma-visao-para-apoio-do-setor-de-bares-e-restaurantes)). An MEI may hire only one employee ([Jornal Contábil](https://jornalcontabil.com.br/noticia/mei-pode-contratar-funcionario-entenda-como-funciona/)). Sebrae (RAIS 2024) puts "restaurants and similar" as the largest small-business employer in the country, with more than 721 thousand formal jobs ([Agência Sebrae](https://agenciasebrae.com.br/dados/micro-e-pequenas-empresas-respondem-por-70-de-todos-os-empregos-formais-do-setor-de-comercio-no-brasil/)). PAS 2024 shows food as the largest services employer (1.86 million people in covered companies, paid 1.4 minimum wages on average) ([Agência Gov](https://agenciagov.ebc.com.br/noticias/202608/em-2024-setor-de-servicos-pagou-r-r-644-2-bi-em-salarios-para-15-9-milhoes-de-pessoas)). Abrasel says 93% of bars and restaurants hire people without experience ([Monitor Mercantil](https://monitormercantil.com.br/abrasel-93-do-setor-de-bares-e-restaurantes-empregam-pessoas-sem-experiencia/), headline-level). Survey samples skew larger: a 2020 Sebrae/Abrasel survey had 32% MEI, 28% ME and 37% EPP ([Agência Sebrae](https://agenciasebrae.com.br/arquivo/empresarios-do-ramo-de-alimentacao-esperam-retorno-lento-dos-clientes-aos-estabelecimentos/)).

**[ANALYSIS]** A rough ratio of the two figures (4.94 million workers ÷ 1.38 million establishments) gives about **3.6 people per establishment, owners included**. It combines two sources and is not a statistic. With 65% MEI, the most common decision unit is the owner alone or the owner plus one helper or family member, so in the modal establishment the owner, buyer, cook, chapeiro and RDC 216 responsible person are the same one to three people. Hired chefs and buyers concentrate in the small share of non-micro establishments and in chains. One consequence links regulation and packaging: where the owner is also the legally responsible handler, the person choosing the mayonnaise is also the one who answers for storage temperature and the expiry of opened packs.

### 3.3 Who decides what, by operator type

**[FACT]** Public evidence is solid only at the two ends: chains (central approval, quality and procurement teams, field consultants who audit standards) and MEI or micro operations (one person does everything). The franchise field consultant (consultor de campo) links franchisor and units, "ensuring standardisation of processes and compliance with rules" through periodic visits, and many franchisees associate the role with training and audits ([Infojobs – consultor de campo](https://www.infojobs.com.br/vaga-de-consultor-campo-rede-franquias-em-sao-paulo__11608653.aspx); [Portal do Franchising](https://www.portaldofranchising.com.br/videos-do-franchising/consultoria-de-campo-ferramenta-do-franchising/)). On external influence, the Rio de Janeiro Commercial Association (ACRJ) found in 2023 that operators discover new products through **technical consultant visits (24%)** and **salesperson visits (22%)**, ahead of social media and TV. The survey also asks about receiving free product to test (produto bonificado), and **brand** appears as a criterion for 31%, after quality, price, yield and availability ([ACRJ (PDF)](https://acrj.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Pesquisa-Bares-e-Restaurantes.pdf), snippet, RJ sample, PDF blocked). Sebrae funds standard menu and recipe-card consulting (Sebraetec) ([Datasebrae (PDF)](https://datasebrae.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Elaboracao-de-Cardapio-e-ou-fichas-tecnicas-para-segmentos-de-alimentacao-MMP14062-8.pdf)), and chef consultancies work on cost of goods and recipe cards for restaurant groups ([Forbes Brasil](https://forbes.com.br/forbeslife/2024/09/chef-carla-pernambuco-investe-em-consultoria-gastronomica-para-diversificar-negocios/)).

# PART 4 — SYNTHESIS: OPERATOR TYPES × STAKEHOLDERS × PACKAGING

## Ownership and operating model connect the three layers

**1. Operator types and how they are classified.** **[ANALYSIS]** Officially, operators are described by CNAE (where and for whom food is served) and LC 123 size. Commercially, they are described by format (Crest/IFB), ownership (Galunion, ABF), management model (ABERC) and cuisine (platforms). None of these systems captures the operating model (dining room, delivery-only, multi-brand, mobile), which is the dimension most connected to how mayonnaise is handled. The labels used in the study ("hamburgueria", "lanchonete", "dark kitchen") are commercial, and only "lanchonete" has an official counterpart.

**2. Actors in purchase and use per context.** **[ANALYSIS]** Ownership predicts the decision locus better than cuisine does. In chains, brand and pack are fixed by the franchisor's approval and, in vertically integrated chains, by a central kitchen. In MEI and micro operations, one person decides, buys, stores and often uses. In mid-size independents, the chef's recipe card, the owner's cost veto and the buyer's equivalence rules interact, and that middle band is where public evidence is weakest.

**3. Distribution channels supplying them.** **[ANALYSIS]** Each ownership band has a dominant route, though operators use several at once (earlier report §4.1). Chains are supplied by contract through logistics operators. Mid-size independents lean on specialized distributors and wholesalers with delivery, with RCAs and industry chefs as influencers. Micro operators lean on cash-and-carry, with the supermarket for emergencies. Marketplaces layer price comparison on top of distributor offers.

**4. Packaging available and for which contexts.** **[FACT]** Foodservice-positioned formats are the 3 kg balde, the ~1 kg pouch, the 1.6–3 kg bag and the 7–8 g sachet. Retail formats (squeeze up to 930 g–1 kg, pouches up to 700 g, jars up to 1 kg) reach small kitchens through cash-and-carry. **[ANALYSIS]** The format a kitchen *buys* and the format it *uses at the point of service* (a refilled bottle, a house-portioned pot, a sachet in the delivery bag) are different things, and only the first is visible in listings.

**5. Differences relevant to investigating the mayonnaise journey.** **[ANALYSIS]** Four contrasts carry most of the variation the sources allow us to see: where mayonnaise meets the customer (in the dish, at the table, in the delivery bag, on the buffet); whether it is a base for a house sauce or used as is; how much cold storage and time the operation has; and, in São Paulo, how close the operator is to the individual-portion rule. None of these can be read from a CNAE, a size band or a pack listing.

## Implications for Field Research

This section lists what deserves attention in recruitment, interviews and observation. It does not anticipate findings about operators and does not propose product or packaging solutions.

**Operator differences that deserve attention in recruitment.** The sources support recording, for every recruited operator, the commercial type, the LC 123 size, the ownership form (independent, own chain, franchise), the physical model (dining room, delivery-only, multi-brand, mobile) and a delivery-share band, with the CNAE noted only as a descriptor. Screening should rely on **declared real activity and operating model**, because the CNAE often reflects tax choices and the gap between registered and operating businesses is large. Within hamburguerias, the artisanal, smash and price-led formats differ in patty size, speed and delivery weight, and menu evidence shows "maionese da casa" across all three in Recife. The owner-operator vs. hired-chef contrast maps onto a real structural divide (65% MEI), but mid-size independents with a chef, a buyer and line staff are the least documented band. In São Paulo, the operator's position relative to the individual-portion rule (still using table bottles, already switched, planning a change) is a recruitment descriptor, because the field period (18/11 to 09/12/2026) falls before the 01/02/2027 date reported by most sources. In Recife, no equivalent rule was found, and the operator's main supply route (cash-and-carry, Armazém Mateus-type distribution wholesale, a local distributor) is worth recording, since no Pernambuco foodservice distributor could be verified. Types found in the notes but outside the earlier typology (pastelarias, botecos, shawarma and kebab shops, padarias) can be considered as contrasts where mayonnaise appears on menus.

**Stakeholders relevant for interviews.** Inside the establishment: the owner or owner-operator; the chef or kitchen lead who writes the recipe cards; the buyer or stock-and-purchasing lead where one exists; the chapeiro, assembler and kitchen assistant who open, decant, label and dose; and, in chains, the unit manager and any quality lead. Outside it: a distributor's sales representative (RCA) and a specialized foodservice distributor; a cash-and-carry business-customer manager (Tenda, Assaí, Atacadão, Mateus); a franchisor's procurement or quality lead and a franchise field consultant; an industry chef; a gastronomic or nutrition consultant who writes recipe cards; and, for regulation, a health-surveillance contact in São Paulo and in Recife. The notes support asking each operator separately "who visits you, whom do you buy from, who delivers, where do you buy in an emergency, and what happens when the brand is missing".

**Packaging formats to recognise and investigate.** Researchers should record the operator's own word **and** photograph the pack, the label (weight, shelf life after opening, resin code) and the closure. Pack size should be noted separately from the amount used. The vocabulary key below is built from the sources in Part 1.
