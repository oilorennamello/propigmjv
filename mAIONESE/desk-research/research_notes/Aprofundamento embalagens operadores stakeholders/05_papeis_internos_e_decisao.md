## 1. Official definitions: CBO occupations and regulatory roles (RDC 216, CFN)

### Takeaway
CBO 2002 has distinct codes for chefe de cozinha, cozinheiro geral, auxiliar/ajudante de cozinha, atendente de lanchonete (which includes "chapista de lanchonete") and gerente de restaurante or bar/lanchonete. **"Chapeiro" as such is not a confirmed CBO title.** In food it falls under "chapista de lanchonete" (5134-35). "Chapeiro" job ads are sometimes filed under metalwork, which is a classification pitfall. Under RDC 216/2004, every food service must have a "responsável pelas atividades de manipulação", who can be **the owner** or a designated, trained employee. So in small shops the owner often holds the food-safety role. A nutricionista as responsável técnico (RT) is a CFN requirement for UAN/alimentação coletiva, not for ordinary lanchonetes.

### Inferences
- INTERPRETATION: under RDC 216 the owner-operator of a small lanchonete or hamburgueria is often **also** the legally relevant "responsável pela manipulação". So the person deciding the mayo brand may also be the one who has to answer for storage temperature and the validade of opened packs. That is a possible link between pack choice (bisnaga vs balde, size) and food-safety concerns.
- INTERPRETATION: the CBO "chefe de cozinha" definition formally includes **ficha técnica and stock control**. Where a hired chef exists, they are the natural specifier of ingredients. CBO does not give them purchasing authority over the supplier or commercial terms.
- INTERPRETATION: the nutricionista RT is mandatory in UAN/coletividade (and in CRN-registered food companies), not in typical independent lanchonetes or hamburguerias. In those, any nutritionist is a hired consultant (see §5).
- For recruitment and screening: "chapeiro" in a CTPS may be registered as atendente de lanchonete (5134-35), cozinheiro geral (5132-05) or even a wrong metal code. Ask about the actual function, not the registered title.

### Gaps
- Official CBO pages (cbo.mte.gov.br) and the official RDC 216 text (items 4.12.x and 4.6.7) were not read. Verify before citing.
- No CBO code checked for comprador (generic), almoxarife/estoquista, or "montador de lanches".
- No national rule found that requires a nutricionista in commercial restaurants or lanchonetes. Some states and municipalities may have their own rules (e.g., SP CVS); not checked.

## 2. Role-by-role profile: responsibilities, decisions, influence on brand/pack, relation to mayo

### Takeaway
Job ads show a consistent division of labor in mid-size operations. The **chapeiro/montador** executes the ficha técnica, and sometimes prepares the house sauces, including "preparar maionese". The **auxiliar de cozinha** receives, labels, stores (PVPS) and pre-prepares sauces. The **líder/chefe de cozinha** controls stock, validade, fichas técnicas and shopping lists. The **comprador** (chains or larger groups only) quotes, negotiates and homologates suppliers. The **gerente** runs the unit to targets. Brand choice sits with owner, chef or corporate. Line staff mainly shape usage, waste and pack preference.

## 3. Role accumulation: how many people a typical establishment has

### Takeaway
The sector is overwhelmingly micro: about **1.38 million active bar/restaurant establishments, 65% MEI and ~94–95% microempresas** (FGV EESP/Abrasel, 2024). An MEI may legally have **only one employee**. Total sector employment of about 4.9–5 million with ~40% informality implies, as a rough ratio, **3–4 people per establishment** (owner included). In the modal establishment, then, owner, buyer, cook, chapeiro and food-safety responsável are the **same one to three people**.

Sector worth R$ 416 bi (3.6% of GDP, 2023). ### Inferences
- INTERPRETATION (rough arithmetic, mixed bases): 4.94 M workers ÷ 1.38 M establishments ≈ **3.6 people per establishment**, owners included. It is a ratio of two different sources, not a statistic. In PAS-covered companies (more formal), formal staffing is larger.
- INTERPRETATION: with 65% MEI (owner plus at most 1 employee), the **most common decision unit is the owner alone or the owner plus a family member or one helper**. The quota split "owner-operator vs hired chef" thus matches a real structural divide. Hired chefs are concentrated in the ~5% non-micro establishments and in chains.
- HYPOTHESIS: family businesses (spouse or children) split roles informally: one buys at the atacarejo, another runs the chapa, a third handles the iFood tablet. Brand inertia ("sempre comprei essa") may come from whoever does the shopping trip, not from the kitchen.
- HYPOTHESIS: in micro operations, the shopping trip itself (Assaí/Atacadão, see note 02) is where the brand decision is made, often by the owner at the shelf, comparing price per kg and size.

### Gaps
- No Sebrae or Abrasel statistic found on the % of owners who **work in the operation** (cook or serve). Sebrae 2016/2017 PDFs may have it but were not readable.
- No RAIS/CNAE breakdown (5611-2/01 restaurantes, 5611-2/03 lanchonetes) by employee band for SP or Recife.
- No data on family ownership share in AFH.

## 4. How decision organization differs by operator type

### Takeaway
Public evidence is solid only at the two ends. At one end are **chains** (centralized production or homologation; quality and procurement teams; field consultants who audit standards). At the other are **MEI/micro operations** (one person does everything). Independent hamburguerias, dark kitchens and food trucks lack direct public evidence on *who chooses the mayo brand*, so most cells are hypotheses for the field. Two chain-specific points emerge. Madero runs a **Cozinha Central** that produces sauces for the whole network. Habib's group (Gennius) **entered recuperação judicial in Aug/2026**, which may affect supplier relations.

## 5. External influencers on ingredient and brand choice

### Takeaway
Besides the industry chef (UFS model, already in note 02), the evidence points to the **vendedor/consultor técnico** as a meaningful source of product discovery: in an ACRJ survey, visits by a consultor técnico (24%) and vendedor (22%) outrank social media. Sebrae funds **menu and ficha-técnica consulting** (Sebraetec). Since the ficha técnica is where an ingredient gets "locked in", external gastronomic consultants and nutritionists can fix the brand indirectly. In franchises, the **consultor de campo** enforces standards but does not choose them. No evidence was found that delivery-app account managers influence ingredient brands.

### Cited Findings
- ACRJ "Pesquisa Bares e Restaurantes" (2023): operators discover new products via **consultor técnico visit 24%** and **vendedor visit 22%**, ahead of social media and TV. The survey also asks about receiving **produto bonificado** to test. Choice criteria include quality and price (top), then rendimento and availability, and also delivery, logistics reliability, payment flexibility and **marca (31%)**. The chart layout reportedly distinguishes roles (influencia / define fornecedor / coloca o pedido), but the mapping was unclear in the snippet — [ACRJ – Pesquisa Bares e Restaurantes (PDF)](https://acrj.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Pesquisa-Bares-e-Restaurantes.pdf) [snippet; PDF blocked; Rio de Janeiro sample; verify]
- Sebraetec has standard consulting products "Elaboração de Cardápio e/ou fichas técnicas para segmentos de alimentação" — [Datasebrae – ficha Sebraetec MMP14062](https://datasebrae.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Elaboracao-de-Cardapio-e-ou-fichas-tecnicas-para-segmentos-de-alimentacao-MMP14062-8.pdf) [snippet]. Sebrae-PR presents the ficha técnica as the basis for knowing each dish's profit contribution — [Sebrae PR – ficha técnica](https://www.sebraepr.com.br/comunidade/artigo/ficha-tecnica-o-que-e-e-por-que-um-restaurante-precisa-ter) [snippet]
- Chef consultancies exist (e.g., Carla Pernambuco's Kitchen Factory). Consultants describe work on CMV, fichas técnicas and team training for restaurant groups — [Forbes Brasil, set/2024](https://forbes.com.br/forbeslife/2024/09/chef-carla-pernambuco-investe-em-consultoria-gastronomica-para-diversificar-negocios/); [Saipos – consultoria para restaurante](https://saipos.com/restaurante/consultoria-para-restaurante) [snippet]
- Nutritionist consulting services listed for restaurants include portion standardization, shopping lists, allergen control and **avaliação de fornecedores** — found in search results with a mismatched title/URL, so unreliable [snippet; low confidence; no URL recorded as trustworthy]
- For distributor reps (RCA, vendedor) and UFS chefs, see note 02 §1 and the report §2.2 (UFS "Chef Gerador de Demanda" works with the distributor's vendedor at "clientes formadores de opinião").

### Inferences
- INTERPRETATION: the **distributor rep directly influences the brand** when (a) the operator buys by delivery rather than at the atacarejo; (b) the spec is open ("maionese", not "maionese X"); (c) there is a bonificação or a trial sample; (d) the rep's mix/margin favors a brand; (e) an industry chef visits jointly. These are conditions to probe in the field.
- HYPOTHESIS: the contador influences cash and payment-term choices (MEI limits, CMV) but not brands. The delivery-app account manager influences menu (combos, photos, packaging for sauces) but not the ingredient brand. Neither has evidence.
- HYPOTHESIS: in chains, the "food engineer"/P&D at the franchisor and the industry's key-account team (with co-development of custom sauces) are the real deciders. This is consistent with note 02, but no Brazilian public case was found for mayo.

### Gaps
- The ACRJ PDF needs to be read in full (role-split question).
- No public Brazilian evidence on bonificação practices for mayo, or on distributor sales-incentive schemes.
- No evidence on delivery-app account managers and ingredients.

## 6. Inputs for Map B (decision & use ecosystem)

### Takeaway
Map B can be built in five layers: **decide → influence → buy → use → sell/supply**, plus a "can determine/restrict brand" overlay. The overlay holds mainly the franchisor (homologation, central kitchen), the chef via ficha técnica, the owner via cash/price, and, indirectly, the norm (RDC 216 / CVS) via storage and validade constraints on pack choice.

### Cited Findings
- Homologation and audits by quality analysts and field consultants (§2, §4); chef's formal ficha técnica and stock role (CBO 2711-05, §1); owner as legal responsável for handling (RDC 216 via secondary sources, §1); 65% MEI / 1-employee cap (§3); vendor and consultor visits as discovery channels (ACRJ, §5) — sources as cited above.

### Inferences (draft Map B; INTERPRETATION/HYPOTHESIS)
| Layer | Independent micro (MEI) | Independent mid | Chain/franchise |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Decides brand** | Owner | Chef/líder (spec) + owner (cost veto) | Franchisor P&D/quality + procurement |
| **Can determine/restrict** | Cash/price at the shelf | Ficha técnica; comprador's equivalence rules | Homologated list; Cozinha Central; audits |
| **Influences** | Atacarejo shelf/promo; distributor rep; peers; chapeiro feedback | Industry chef (UFS-type); distributor rep; gastronomic consultant; nutritionist consultant | Industry key account; co-development; consultor de campo (enforces) |
| **Buys** | Owner (trip to Assaí/Atacadão/Mateus) | Comprador/gerente/líder (lists, WhatsApp orders) | Franchisee/gerente orders from a homologated distributor |
| **Receives/stores** | Owner | Auxiliar/estoquista (PVPS, validade) | Crew per manual |
| **Uses daily** | Owner + helper | Chapeiro, montador, auxiliar (sauce batches) | Crew (dispensers, sachês) |
| **Sells/supplies** | Atacarejo, supermarket (emergency) | Distributor/atacado with delivery, B2B apps | Exclusive distributor/logistics operator |
| **Consumes** | Client (sees bisnaga/sachê brand) | Client (brand hidden in house sauce) | Client (brand on sachê, if any) |

- Field checks for the quota "owner-operator vs hired chef": (1) who physically buys; (2) who wrote the ficha técnica; (3) whether a substitute brand is accepted when there is a ruptura, and who decides; (4) who is the RDC 216 responsável; (5) who the chapeiro complains to about a mayo; (6) last time a rep, industry chef or consultant suggested a brand.

### Gaps
- All of Map B beyond chains vs MEI structure is unverified in public sources. It is to be validated by the 18 ethnographies (SP 10, Recife 8). No Recife-specific evidence on role organization was found.
