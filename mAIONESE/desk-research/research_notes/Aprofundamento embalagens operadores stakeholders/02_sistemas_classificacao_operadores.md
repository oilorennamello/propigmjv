## Q1. Official classifications (IBGE CNAE 2.3, MEI, porte/LC 123, IBGE PAS and POF)

### Takeaway
The only official taxonomy of operators is the **CNAE 2.3 (IBGE/CONCLA), Division 56 "Alimentação"**, inside Seção I "Alojamento e alimentação". It classifies by **form of service/production**: consumption on premises, ambulant, prepared for companies, buffet, cantina, prepared for home consumption. It does not classify by cuisine, brand, chain/independent or channel. Size (*porte*) is a separate legal axis (LC 123: MEI/ME/EPP by revenue). Padarias sit **outside Division 56** (indústria 10.91 or comércio 47.21). Hamburgueria, pizzaria, dark kitchen and food truck have **no CNAE of their own**; they fall into generic subclasses.

### Inferences
- The CNAE logic is **"where and for whom the food is served"**: on premises (5611), ambulant (5612), for companies, events, private cantinas or home consumption (5620). Within 5611 the split is **meal vs. snack vs. drinks**. A hamburgueria artesanal can legitimately be 5611-2/01 or 5611-2/03. A delivery-only kitchen can be 5611-2/03, 5611-2/01 or 5620-1/04. **The CNAE does not reveal the operating model.** [ANALYTICAL]
- Because a CNPJ has one **main** CNAE plus several **secondary** CNAEs, and the choice is often driven by **tax and MEI eligibility** (and not by the real mix), CNAE counts (RAIS, Receita) over- or under-state segments. IFB/Driva found 1.459 million active foodservice CNPJs in 2022, of which only 736 thousand were actually operating ([Mercado & Consumo, 15/07/2022 – snippet](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/15/07/2022/artigos/raio-x-do-foodservice-no-brasil/)). [ANALYTICAL + V1]
- Implication for recruitment: screen by **declared real activity and operating model**, not by CNAE. [ANALYTICAL]

## Q2. Sectoral / commercial classifications (IFB, Crest/Gouvêa, Galunion, Abrasel/Sebrae, ABF, ABIA, ABERC)

### Takeaway
Each Brazilian institution segments the channel by what it measures. **IFB/Crest** segments by **consumer-facing format**: fast food, full service, padarias, lanchonetes, quilo/buffet, varejo/conveniência. **Galunion** segments by **ownership** (redes vs. independentes vs. outros) and **service model** (serviço completo vs. serviço rápido vs. padarias). **Abrasel/Sebrae** surveys use **establishment type × service mode**. **ABF** uses franchise segment and subsegment. **ABERC** uses the **management model** of collective feeding (autogestão vs. terceirizada: concessão, refeição transportada, convênio). **ABIA** has a binary *varejo alimentar vs. food service*. None of these is published as a formal public glossary (or at least none was found).

### Inferences
- There are **three families of sector taxonomy**: (1) *format/consumer occasion* (Crest/IFB: fast food, full service, padaria, lanchonete, quilo/buffet, conveniência); (2) *ownership* (Galunion, ABF: rede/franquia vs. independente); (3) *contract/management* (ABERC: autogestão vs. concessão). They are orthogonal and are usually reported separately. [ANALYTICAL]
- "Lanchonete" is the one term that exists in **all** systems (CNAE 5611-2/03, MEI occupation, Driva count, Crest channel, Sebrae survey type). It is the most robust bridge category for the study. "Hamburgueria" appears only in commercial/platform data (iFood, WISE). [ANALYTICAL]
- "Padaria" is a full foodservice channel for Crest (534 million transactions), but **not** foodservice in CNAE. That is a key mismatch for any sizing. [ANALYTICAL]

## Q3. International frameworks used by multinationals (Euromonitor, Technomic, Circana)

### Takeaway
Multinationals typically use (a) **commercial vs. non-commercial** (Technomic): non-commercial means foodservice embedded in an organisation whose main business isn't food, often subsidised. And (b) **Euromonitor "Consumer Foodservice"** outlet types: Cafés/Bars, Full-Service Restaurants, Limited-Service Restaurants, Self-Service Cafeterias, Street Stalls/Kiosks, cross-cut by **chained vs. independent** and by **service channel** (dine-in, takeaway, delivery, drive-through). Exact Euromonitor definitions were not retrieved in this session.

### Cited Findings
- Euromonitor's consumer foodservice reports list the categories Cafés/Bars, Full-Service Restaurants, Limited-Service Restaurants and Self-Service Cafeterias (US report listing). Street Stalls/Kiosks appears in other country reports. [V1] — [Euromonitor – Consumer Foodservice in the US](https://www.euromonitor.com/consumer-foodservice-in-the-us/report); [MarketResearch – Euromonitor food service listings](https://marketresearch.com/Euromonitor-International-v746/Food-Service-Hospitality-c169/8.html)
- Technomic **non-commercial** scope: colleges & universities, K-12, healthcare, business & industry (B&I), military, lodging, recreation, travel, retail meal solutions, convenience stores and travel centers. Non-commercial was ~34% of the US industry in 2012. [V1–V2] — [Meatingplace](https://meatingplace.com/technomic-noncommercial-foodservice-growth-steady/); [PR Newswire – Technomic](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/noncommercial-foodservice-showing-signs-of-growth-says-technomic-192289941.html)
- Definition (textbook): non-commercial operations are embedded in an organisation where F&B is not the primary business and are typically subsidised. [V1] — [Penn State Pressbooks](https://psu.pb.unizin.org/hmd329/?p=27); [eCampus Ontario](https://ecampusontario.pressbooks.pub/principlesofmanagementinnutrition/?p=81)
- Academic restaurant segments (Canziani et al. 2016, cited in a Brazilian paper): fast food, fast casual, midscale, moderate/casual (then fine dining). [V1] — [core.ac.uk](https://core.ac.uk/download/303960175.pdf)

### Inferences
- Mapping international classes onto Brazilian ones [ANALYTICAL]:
  - LSR/QSR ≈ Crest "fast food" + most lanchonetes + hamburguerias + pizzarias de balcão/delivery.
  - FSR ≈ Crest "full service" (à la carte, rodízio).
  - **Self-service cafeterias ≈ the Brazilian "por quilo"/buffet**, a very large local format that does not fit US categories well.
  - Cafés/Bars ≈ bares, botecos, cafeterias, and partly padarias.
  - Street stalls/kiosks ≈ CNAE 5612-1/00 ambulantes/food trucks.
  - Non-commercial ≈ ABERC refeições coletivas (CNAE 5620-1/01, 5620-1/03).
- The Brazilian **padaria** is a hybrid (bakery retail + café + lanchonete + sometimes por-quilo lunch) with no clean international equivalent. [ANALYTICAL]

- Euromonitor's and Technomic's official definitions (glossary/methodology pages) were not retrieved. Circana (ex-NPD) Brazil category definitions were not found; Crest in Brazil now appears under Gouvêa Inteligência/IFB.
- No Brazil-specific Euromonitor share of chained vs. independent outlets found (paid).

## Q5. Overlap: which classifications are mutually exclusive and which coexist, with a proposed overlap matrix

### Takeaway
Within a single system, categories are **meant to be exclusive**: one main CNAE, one LC 123 porte, one ABERC model per contract. **Across systems, they all coexist.** The same operator can be CNAE 5611-2/03 + ME + franchise + salão & delivery + hamburgueria. Real ambiguity appears in five places: padaria (industry/retail vs. foodservice), delivery-only (5611 vs. 5620-1/04), dark kitchen with virtual brands (one CNPJ, many "restaurants" on apps), food truck (5612 vs. fixed point, single vs. multi-unit), and CNAE-as-declared vs. real activity.

### Cited Findings
- One CNPJ can combine a main and secondary CNAEs, e.g. 5620-1/04 main + 5620-1/03 secondary; a lanchonete with heavy drink sales registers 5611-2/03 + 5611-2/04. — [CNPJá example](https://cnpja.com/office/32626892000127); [VRI Consulting](https://vriconsulting.com.br/cnaes/atividades-permitidas-microempreendedor-individual-mei.php?pagina=13)
- Official exclusion notes make some codes mutually exclusive: 5611-2/03 excludes ambulante (5612-1/00); 5611-2/01 excludes bufê (5620-1/02); 5620-1/04 excludes 5611-2/01, 5620-1/02 and 5620-1/03; 4721-1/02 excludes 1091-1/02. — [SES-MG (PDF)](https://www.saude.mg.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/11-10-2024-Atividades-Nivel-de-Risco-I-sujeito-ao-controle-sanitario-1-291.pdf); [BuscadorNCM 5620104](https://buscadorncm.com.br/cnae/5620104); [Joinville (PDF)](https://www.joinville.sc.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Parecer-Sanitario-Atividades-economicas-dos-estabelecimentos-de-fabricacao-de-produtos-de-padaria-e-confeitaria.pdf)

### Inferences
**Exclusivity rules [ANALYTICAL]**
- *Mutually exclusive by design:* main CNAE (one per CNPJ); porte LC 123 (one at a time); MEI vs. ME/EPP; within a contract, autogestão vs. concessão; Crest "channel" assignment of a given transaction.
- *Coexisting by nature:* service channels (salão + balcão + delivery + retirada); ownership forms within one brand (rede própria + franquias, e.g. stores owned and franchised); cuisine (multi-brand kitchens); commercial and institutional (a concessionaire that also runs commercial restaurants).

- The **type** label (column 1) predicts the CNAE only weakly. It predicts **channels** better.
