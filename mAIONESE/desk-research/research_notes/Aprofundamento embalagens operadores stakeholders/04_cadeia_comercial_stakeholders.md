## 1. Taxonomy: sales channel × commercial model × company type (and the ABAD/ABAAS definitions)

### Takeaway
Three things must not be confused. The **channel** is the route the product travels to the kitchen. The **commercial model** is how the intermediary sells and serves: with delivery, cash & carry, a sales force (RCA), televendas or an app. The **company type** is the legal/business identity. One company often runs several models at once: Tenda (store + televendas + atacado distribuidor with 200+ reps), Grupo Mateus (atacarejo + atacado de distribuição "Armazém" + varejo) and Atacadão (cash & carry + B2B delivery). ABAD (Associação Brasileira de Atacadistas e Distribuidores) classifies the indirect channel by commercial model. ABAAS (atacado de autosserviço/cash & carry) split from ABAD and rejects the label "atacarejo".

### Cited Findings
- **ABAD models.** ABAD classifies the indirect channel by business model: distribuidor (com entrega), atacado generalista com entrega, atacado (generalista) de autosserviço, and atacado de balcão. 2015 survey: distribuidor 43.3%, atacado com entrega 31.9%, atacado de autosserviço 19.3%. Another breakdown (base excluding Atacadão): atacado generalista com entrega 35.6% and atacado generalista de autosserviço 13.1% — [Bandamazonas (repr. ABAD data)](https://bandamazonas.com.br/?p=23778) [S, V2]. The 2025 ranking figures (distribuidor 45.5%, generalista com entrega 35.3%, autosserviço 13.8%, balcão 4.2%) are already in file 02. The ABAD PDF is still unread ([ABAD PDF](https://abad.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Apresentacao-Nielsen_final.pdf), fetch blocked again).
- **No official ABAD glossary definition of "atacado generalista" or "distribuidor" was retrievable.** The ABAD president's interview on Giro News records industry complaints about the atacado distribuidor: speculative negotiations, unfair margins and exclusivity policies — [Giro News – presidente da ABAD](https://gironews.com/eventos-especiais/presidente-da-abad-fala-sobre-nova-perspectiva-para-o-atacado-21389/) [S, V2].
- **Atacarejo vs. atacado.** A search summary attributes to ABAD the criterion that an "atacarejo" is a store where more than 50% of sales go to pessoas físicas. The originating article was not identified (candidates: [IstoÉ Dinheiro 2014 – Questão tributária divide atacadistas e atacarejos](https://www.istoedinheiro.com.br/questao-tributaria-divide-atacadistas-e-atacarejos/); [Exame – Após conflito, atacarejos se unem e criam nova associação](https://exame.com/economia/apos-conflito-atacarejos-se-unem-e-criam-nova-associacao/)) [S, V3: attribution not confirmed].
- **ABAAS origin.** ABAAS was created by self-service wholesale chains after a conflict inside ABAD. One trigger was ABAD's push to make the buyer's CPF mandatory on the atacarejo invoice. One founder said: "Nós somos cash & carry ou atacado de autosserviço. 'Atacarejo' não nos define" — [Exame](https://exame.com/economia/apos-conflito-atacarejos-se-unem-e-criam-nova-associacao/); [Giro News – cash & carry cria associação](https://gironews.com/atacado-cash-carry/cash-carry-cria-associacao-28738/); [Giro News – Roldão: "vamos definir precisamente o modelo cash & carry"](https://gironews.com/atacado-cash-carry/roldao-vamos-definir-precisamente-o-modelo-cash-carry-24187/) [S, V1 – several outlets]. The ABAAS books are online (e.g., [Book ABAAS 15](https://abaas.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/book_abaas_15_final-site-2.pdf)) but were not read.
- **Distributor's economic role (Brazilian contract model).** In a contrato de distribuição/concessão mercantil, the distributor is the merchant in the "difusão" phase between manufacturer and the wholesale/retail trade. It is paid by the spread between purchase price and resale price, i.e., it **buys and resells**. It is not paid a commission — [Law Insider – Contrato de distribuição e concessão mercantil (PT)](https://lawinsider.com/pt/contracts/fMPzySL7TAt) [S, V3 – template, not case law]. Territorial exclusivity is usually bilateral (the distributor does not invoice outside its zone, and the manufacturer neither names another distributor nor sells direct there) and is often conditional on purchase targets. This was found only in non-Brazilian, generic sources — [Universidad Externado (PDF)](https://revistas.uexternado.edu.co/index.php/contexto/article/download/1799/1606/6140) [S, V3; not Brazil-specific].
- **Multi-channel companies (examples):**
  - **Tenda Atacado (SP, Guarulhos):** four formats: physical stores, **televendas** for comerciantes, **atacado distribuidor** with more than 200 sales representatives, and e-commerce — [Grandes Nomes da Propaganda – "Tá no Tenda, Tá na Mão"](https://grandesnomesdapropaganda.com.br/anunciantes/tenda-atacado-lanca-campanha-ta-no-tenda-ta-na-mao/); [Marcas pelo Mundo](https://marcaspelomundo.com.br/anunciantes/tenda-atacado-lanca-campanha-ta-no-tenda-ta-na-mao/) [S, V2]. Note: ri.tenda.com belongs to Construtora Tenda (real estate), **not** Tenda Atacado.
  - **Grupo Mateus:** varejo, atacarejo (Mix Mateus → Novo Atacarejo) and **atacado de distribuição/B2B**. The B2B division had revenue of **R$ 2.1 bi in 3Q25 (+26.8%)** and served **>48 thousand customers/month** from its DC network. When entering a new city, the group first uses the atacado model (Armazém Mateus), with **almost 2,000 representantes comerciais**. That figure comes from a sell-side report of uncertain date — [BP Money](https://bpmoney.com.br/negocios/grupo-mateus-fecha-acordo-com-concorrente-e-avanca-no-nordeste); [XP – Grupo Mateus, uma joia garimpada no Nordeste](https://conteudos.xpi.com.br/acoes/relatorios/grupo-mateus-gmat3-uma-joia-garimpada-no-nordeste-iniciamos-com-recomendacao-de-compra/); [Genial – IoC Grupo Mateus (PDF, 2023)](https://media-genial-analisa.genialinvestimentos.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/29181342/IoC-Genial-Grupo-Mateus-GMAT3-2.pdf) [S, V2]. The Novo Atacarejo deal combines "varejo, atacado de distribuição e atacarejo" in PE/PB/AL — [BP Money](https://bpmoney.com.br/negocios/grupo-mateus-fecha-acordo-com-concorrente-e-avanca-no-nordeste) [S, V2].
  - **Atacadão (Carrefour):** cash & carry stores with a B2B base whose volumes drove 1Q25 growth (see §4).

### Inferences
- INTERPRETAÇÃO: Map A should code each arrow by **commercial model**, not by company name: "com entrega/RCA", "autosserviço/cash & carry", "televendas/WhatsApp", "app/marketplace" and "contrato/homologado". A company name then appears in several boxes. Tenda in SP and Mateus in PE are the clearest cases.
- INTERPRETAÇÃO: the ABAD "distribuidor" (often with territorial or brand arrangements with the industry) and the "atacado generalista com entrega" (broad multi-brand assortment) should be kept apart. The distribuidor is where a manufacturer such as Kraft Heinz can secure availability of a specific pack. The generalista carries what turns.

### Gaps
- No official ABAD/ABAAS text with formal definitions could be read (fetch blocked). The "more than 50% to pessoa física" criterion is unconfirmed.
- No ABAD data found on the share of the indirect channel's revenue sold to foodservice customers.

## 4. Atacadistas (with delivery) vs. atacarejos/cash & carry vs. clube vs. supermarket

### Takeaway

- Assaí is rolling out **Assaí Pay** for B2B and B2C (to the end of 2026).
- **Roldão** (about 40 stores, SP only, Nov/2025) and **Spani** (42 stores SP/RJ, mid-2024) are confirmed atacarejos in SP, but their PJ/foodservice offer was **not** verified.

## 9. Influence matrix: how each intermediary shapes availability, price, volume, frequency and substitution (inputs for Map A)

- **Approved lists (franchisor + logistics operator)** freeze brand and pack and eliminate substitution.
- **Distributors and RCAs** filter which brands and packs reach the independent, and offer credit.

- **Marketplaces** increase price comparison.
- **Supermarkets** take emergency purchases in retail packs.
