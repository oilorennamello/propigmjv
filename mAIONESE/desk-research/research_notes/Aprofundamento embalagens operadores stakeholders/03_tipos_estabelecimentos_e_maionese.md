## Q0. Cross-cutting: what is new versus the first round

### Takeaway
Across almost every type, menus show the same pattern: mayo appears either as a **named house sauce** ("maionese da casa", "maionese especial", "maionese verde", "maionese de alho", "maionese defumada") or as a **sold extra** (sachê or pote, priced separately). This round also adds three things. Chains (BK, Giraffas, McDonald's) are **using mayo as a merchandised product**. Institutional school feeding (PNAE) **prohibits buying mayonnaise** with program funds. Buffet mayo salads are a **documented food-safety hotspot**.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT] In SP the shared-bisnaga ban covers "bares, padarias, lanchonetes e restaurantes". Condiments for sandwiches and salgados must be in individual, single-use portions; **sachê is not mandatory**. The start date was moved from 04/10/2026 to **01/02/2027** (CVS 9/2026) after Abrasel-SP asked for more time over financial and operational impact. Coverage notes that sachês "costumam ter um custo maior por quantidade de produto" and that Abrasel says the change needs new equipment and training. A baker interviewed by Band said she already uses only sachês because she considers them more hygienic. — [Band (snippet)](https://www.band.com.br/nacional/sp/sp-proibe-bisnagas-de-molho-em-bares-e-lanchonetes); [Band – adiamento (snippet)](https://www.band.com.br/economia/governo-de-sao-paulo-adia-proibicao-de-bisnagas-de-molho); [Correio da Manhã (snippet)](https://www.correiodamanha.com.br/nacional/sao-paulo/2026/10/325192-sp-adia-a-proibicao-de-bisnagas-de-molho-para-2027.html); [BaresSP (snippet)](https://baressp.com.br/noticias/proibicao-das-bisnagas-de-molhos-e-adiada-para-2027); [Mercado & Consumo 06/10/2026 (snippet)](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/06/10/2026/foodservice/bares-e-restaurantes-de-sp-ganham-prazo-maior-para-fim-das-bisnagas-de-molho/amp/)
- [FACT] Menus show sachês sold as paid extras: a pizzaria in Salvador sells "sachê de maionese" for R$ 0.99; a Belo Horizonte pizzaria sells "Maionese Sachê 7 Gramas – 2 Unidades" for R$ 1.00; Burger King stores in BH, RJ, Guarulhos and Goiânia list a maionese sachê at R$ 3.99. — [99Food – Pizzaria Inovação, Salvador (snippet)](https://99app.com/99food/salvador/pizzaria-inovaao/5764608449229620463/); [99Food – Pizzaria Copacabana, BH (snippet)](https://99app.com/99food/belo-horizonte/pizzaria-copacabana/5764608591622046898/); [99Food – Burger King Guarulhos (snippet)](https://99app.com/99food/sao-paulo/burger-king-fs-guarulhos-otav-braga-mesquit-3450/5764612955766000241/)

### Inferences
- [INTERPRETATION] "Maionese avulsa" (sold as a priced extra) is visible across segments as small, explicit prices (R$ 0.99 to R$ 4.90). Some operators treat mayo as a **revenue line**, not only a cost. This matters for pack choice: the sachê is both a service unit and a sellable SKU.
- [HYPOTHESIS] The SP transition date (01/02/2027) falls after the field period. The SP visits will probably find mixed practice: bisnagas still on counters, plus some early adopters of sachês or potinhos. Ask what each operator **plans** to do and what cost they expect.

### Gaps
- No public study quantifies the cost difference between bisnaga, sachê and house-portioned potinhos for an operator.

## Q1. Lanchonete (incl. lanchonete de bairro, "X" lanches, sanduicherias)

### Takeaway
The lanchonete is the broadest, most informal category. Its kitchen centres on a **chapa** plus a montagem bench. Mayo is used two ways: applied in the X-lanches (x-salada, x-tudo) and served at table, historically from a **bisnaga**, including the house "maionese verde". The SP ban hits exactly this service model. No official Sebrae page on lanchonete structure could be read.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT] Startup guides (not Sebrae primary) estimate R$ 6–20 thousand for structure and equipment of a small lanchonete. They recommend multifunctional, compact equipment, for example "uma chapa que também funcione como sanduicheira", laid out "seguindo o fluxo de trabalho". Licences come from the prefeitura, bombeiros and vigilância sanitária. — [Saipos – montar lanchonete (snippet)](https://saipos.com/sistema/lanchonete/montar-lanchonete); [Saipos – equipamentos (snippet)](https://saipos.com/lanchonete/equipamentos-para-lanchonete); [Stone (snippet)](https://conteudo.stone.com.br/como-montar-uma-lanchonete/)
- [FACT] The "maionese verde de lanchonete" (milk + oil + herbs, no egg) and customers trying to take the bisnaga were already documented in the first round. — see first-round notes ([Gazeta SP](https://www.gazetasp.com.br/gazeta-mais/o-molho-verde-que-faz-o-x-salada-parecer-de-hamburgueria-e-muda-o-lanche-em-minutos/))
- [FACT] A Palmas (TO) study found Salmonella in 85.72% of samples of homemade mayo sold in lanchonetes. Homemade mayo has pH ≥ 4.2, which favours bacterial growth (local sample; do not generalise). Coverage citing Anvisa says homemade mayo should not stay at room temperature for more than 30 minutes. — [SET periódicos (snippet)](https://periodicos.set.edu.br/saude/article/download/11531/5494/35163); [Viva (snippet)](https://viva.com.br/saude-e-bem-estar/maionese-pode-fazer-mal-estudo-revela-alto-risco-de-contaminacao.html)
- [FACT] Recife: Cascatinha Lanches (centro) is a traditional lanchonete known for cachorro-quente. One user review said the hot dog lacked flavour and needed extra ketchup, maionese and pimenta. This shows table condiments are part of service there (individual opinion). — [Wanderlog – Cascatinha (snippet)](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/3727782/cascatinha-lanches)

### Inferences
- [INTERPRETATION] **Differentiation criterion:** the lanchonete is defined by a short, fast menu of sandwiches and salgados at low ticket, cooked on a chapa or fryer and served at a counter. It is set apart from the hamburgueria by **breadth** (lanches + salgados + sucos + sometimes PF) and lower culinary positioning.
- [HYPOTHESIS] **Roles:** in a lanchonete de bairro the owner often buys (atacarejo), cooks on the chapa and handles the till. Family members or 1–3 staff do montagem and the counter. A house mayo is probably made by the owner or the most senior staff member.
- [HYPOTHESIS] **Mayo flow:** balde or bag → refilled bisnaga (kitchen and table) → application "no olho" (by eye). Plain mayo and house green mayo may coexist. In SP from 02/2027, the table bisnaga must be replaced by individual portions.
- [HYPOTHESIS] **Pack logic:** price per kg and yield dominate. The bisnaga is the operating unit, whatever the purchase pack is.

### Gaps
- Sebrae "ideias de negócio – lanchonete" could not be read (domain not resolvable). No public data on grams of mayo per X-lanche.

## Q2. Cachorro-quente / dogueiro (carrinho, trailer, delivery hot dog, "prensado")

### Takeaway
Cachorro-quente is a strong mayo vehicle in both cities. Recife menus explicitly list "maionese da casa"/"maionese especial da casa" among many toppings. Street carts depend on small, mobile, poorly refrigerated set-ups, and homemade mayo from carts is the most studied food-safety risk. No Recife-specific press history of trailer or cart hot dog culture was found.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT] Sebrae-MG has a "Carrinho de cachorro-quente" business sheet. A snippet suggests offering "variações como queijo e presunto ralado ou um molho diferente" (the sauce as differentiator). The PDF could not be read. — [Sebrae MG – Carrinho de Cachorro-quente (snippet)](https://www.sebrae.com.br/Sebrae/Portal%20Sebrae/UFs/MG/Imagens/Carrinho%20de%20Cachorro-quente.pdf)
- [FACT] Assaí (cash-and-carry) runs an "Academia Assaí" course module, "Aula 06 – equipamentos e embalagens – dogueiros". The wholesaler treats dogueiros as a distinct client profile (content not readable). — [Academia Assaí PDF (title only)](https://www.academiaassai.com.br/sites/default/files/aula_06_-_resumo_equipamentos_e_embalagens_dogueiros.pdf)
- [FACT] Recife delivery menu: the Minute Hot Dog "tradicional" lists vinagrete, milho e ervilha, batata palha, queijo ralado, ketchup, mostarda and "maionese especial da casa" (already in round 1). — [99Food – Minute Hot Dog Recife](https://99app.com/99food/recife/minute-hot-dog-entrega-rapida/5764610447152777850/)
- [FACT] In Belém, a 46-year-old street hot dog seller is reported to have added "maionese temperada" as one of his few novelties. This is an example of mayo as differentiator in street hot dog (North, not Recife). — [O Liberal – Cachorro-quente do Mário (snippet)](https://www.oliberal.com/receita/mediciland/cachorro-quente-do-mario-ha-46-anos-nas-ruas-de-belem-1.537374)
- [FACT] Academic work has analysed homemade mayo from hot dog carts for microbiological and parasitological contamination. — [Even3 anais (snippet)](https://static.even3.com/anais/1247836.pdf)
- [FACT] Colatina (ES), Portaria 002/2006, bans homemade mayo also for **vendedores ambulantes**, with penalties including apprehension and destruction of the product, and refers to individual disposable packs (sachês). — [Colatina Portaria 002/2006](https://colatina.es.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Portaria_002-2006.pdf)

### Inferences
- [INTERPRETATION] **Differentiation:** carrinho/ambulante (mobile, minimal structure, usually one person) vs. trailer or fixed point (more toppings, chapa, sometimes seating) vs. delivery hot-dog brand (montagem line, potinhos). The Recife "completo" style with many toppings makes mayo one of several cold sauces, not the protagonist, **except** where the house mayo is the named signature.
- [HYPOTHESIS] Carts work from a caixa térmica or a small refrigerator. The bisnaga stays exposed for hours, so the risk of temperature abuse is highest here. Cart sellers may prefer smaller packs (bisnaga or squeeze, 1 kg bag) that run out within one shift.
- [HYPOTHESIS] "Hot dog prensado" (pressed on the chapa, common in parts of Brazil) puts mayo inside the bread before pressing. This was not verified for SP or Recife.

### Gaps
- No Recife press (JC, Diario de Pernambuco, Folha PE) on the cachorro-quente trailer or cart tradition or its sauces was found. A Diario de Pernambuco hot dog article found is about a Passo Fundo (RS) chain. — [Diario de Pernambuco (snippet)](https://diariodepernambuco.com.br/dpmais/cachorro-quente-amado-pelos-brasileiros-expande-atividades/)
- No news found of Recife VISA seizing homemade mayo from ambulantes.

## Q3. Pastelaria (incl. pastel de feira)

### Takeaway
The pastelaria's anchor condiment is **vinagrete**, but SP-region menus show mayo-based sauces (green, temperada, with relish, garlic) and sachês. Mayo is an accessory, served on the side, not cooked inside the pastel.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT] Pastel de feira originates in São Paulo state, and every SP feira has at least one pastel stall, usually near caldo de cana. — [Wikipedia – Pastel (snippet)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pastel_(Brazilian_food))
- [FACT] Menus: Malibu Pastelaria (SP) sells a green sauce described as mayonnaise with spices. Pastel Chefinhos (SP) lists a "maionese temperada" and a mayo-plus-pickle-relish sauce. Pastelaria Sabor de Feira (Campinas) sells ketchup, mostarda and maionese **in sachês**. Pastel Gourmet Curitiba offers homemade garlic mayo. Senaga (SP) vinagrete is cabbage, onion, tomato and parsley. — [99Food – Malibu (snippet)](https://99app.com/99food/sao-paulo/malibu-pastelaria-pastel-de-feira/5764608543597266154/); [99Food – Chefinhos Paulista (snippet)](https://99app.com/99food/sao-paulo/pastel-chefinhos-paulista/5764608408955913540/); [99Food – Sabor de Feira (snippet)](https://99app.com/99food/campinas/pastelaria-sabor-de-feira/5764615129656985346/); [99Food – Senaga (snippet)](https://99app.com/99food/sao-paulo/pastel-de-feira-senaga/5764610271889590140/)
- [FACT] The CVS 3/2026 individual-portion rule explicitly covers "pastéis" (round 1). — [Mix Vale](https://www.mixvale.com.br/2026/09/30/sao-paulo-proibe-bisnagas-de-molhos-em-bares-e-lanchonetes/)

### Inferences
- [INTERPRETATION] Operating model: fryer-centred, dough and fillings prepped in advance, very fast counter service (feira stalls and fixed shops). Mayo lives at the service point, not in the kitchen. So the CVS rule hits the pastelaria's mayo use **almost completely**.
- [HYPOTHESIS] Feira stalls (outdoor, no fixed refrigeration) are where sachês are most attractive (no cold chain once opened). Fixed pastelarias with delivery may house-portion green mayo in potinhos.

### Gaps
- No source on pastelaria use of mayo in Recife (pastel is less central there; caldo de cana + pastel exists but was not documented).

## Q4. Hamburgueria (artesanal vs. smash vs. chain)

### Takeaway
Round 1 covered market size. This round adds Recife menu evidence. Both artisanal and smash formats in Recife name "maionese da casa" on the burger. In SP, flavoured house mayos (defumada, verde, alho) are common menu language. Smash burgers use thin (around 50 g) patties and fast assembly, which favours ready-to-use sauces in bisnagas.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT] Recife: Bora Burger (hamburgueria artesanal) lists a smash burger with "pão brioche amanteigado, blend bovino smash 50g, cheddar e maionese da casa", a "maionese especial da casa" version and a 120 g beef burger with maionese da casa. Burgaço (Recife) lists burgers with "maionese da casa" but describes patties as "blend bovino industrial 56g". This shows house mayo even in non-artisanal, price-led burgers. — [99Food – Bora Burger Recife (snippet)](https://99app.com/99food/recife/bora-burger-hamburgeria-artesanal/5764608602766312659/); [99Food – Burgaço Recife (snippet)](https://99app.com/99food/recife/burgao/5764610530195802541/)
- [FACT] Recife (Aflitos): Stacker Burguer e Fries opened as a smash-burger specialist working from 17h (Folha PE, image dated 2020). — [Folha PE (snippet)](https://www.folhape.com.br/sabores/zona-norte-ganha-hamburgueria-especializada-em-smash-burguers/165611/)
- [FACT] SP and interior menus with flavoured house mayo: Hamburgueria Açaí Campestre (SP) with "maionese defumada"; Art Burguer (Campinas) with maionese verde; Mr. Smash (SP) with house mayo. — [99Food – Açaí Campestre (snippet)](https://99app.com/99food/sao-paulo/hamburgueria-aai-campestre/5764608347597442825/); [99Food – Art Burguer (snippet)](https://99app.com/99food/campinas/art-burguer/5764610302373790281/); [99Food – Mr. Smash (snippet)](https://99app.com/99food/sao-paulo/mr-smash-hamburguer-milkshake-fritas-e-combos/5764608256648154895/)
- [FACT] Manufacturers target the hamburgueria directly: Hellmann's launched a "Molho para Burger" and expanded its condiment line in Brazil. — [Propmark (snippet)](https://propmark.com.br/anunciantes/hellmanns-lanca-molho-para-burger-e-amplia-portfolio-de-condimentos-no-brasil/); [GIRO News (snippet)](https://gironews.com/lancamentos/hellmanns-amplia-linha-de-molhos-com-versao-para-hamburguer/)

### Inferences
- [INTERPRETATION] **Differentiation:**
  - (a) **Artesanal**: own blend of 120–180 g, brioche, chef or owner-chef, salão + delivery; mayo as the base for several named sauces.
  - (b) **Smash**: thin patties pressed on the chapa, short menu, very high speed, strong delivery; usually one house mayo plus a few flavours.
  - (c) **Chain/franchise**: centrally specified sauces (Q8).
  Price-led "artesanal" burgers (industrial 56 g patty + house mayo) blur (a) and (b). "Maionese da casa" is a **marketing label** used across tiers and does not prove the sauce is made from scratch.
- [HYPOTHESIS] **Kitchen:** chapa + fritadeira + montagem bench with a refrigerated base (balcão refrigerado/pista fria), sauce bisnagas in line, delivery packing station. **Roles:** owner or chef defines the sauce recipe; a chapeiro and a montador (often young, high turnover) apply it; a delivery packer portions potinhos.
- [HYPOTHESIS] **Volume:** mayo per burger is probably higher than in other types (sauce on bread + extra pote in delivery + fries dip). Peaks are concentrated in the evening and on Sunday (iFood data, round 1).

### Gaps
- No public data on grams of mayo per burger, or on how many hamburguerias make their mayo from scratch vs. flavouring industrial mayo.

## Q5. Restaurante independente (à la carte; PF/prato feito/comercial)

### Takeaway
In lunch restaurants mayo is mostly an **ingredient** (salada de maionese, salpicão, molhos frios), not a condiment. Per round 1 and CVS, meals (pratos) are outside the SP individual-portion rule. Little direct evidence on PF kitchens was found this round.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT] CVS 3/2026 individual-portion rule targets sandwiches, salgados, pastéis and similar. Buffet, self-service and à la carte meals are outside it (round 1, CRN-3 consensus). — [CRN-3 consenso (PDF)](https://crn3.org.br/arquivos/consenso-portaria-cvs3-2026-17-09-26pdf.pdf)
- [FACT] Giraffas (a chain, but a "prato" format) lets diners choose "um molho especial Giraffas" with steak plates. This shows cold sauces accompany pratos in the restaurant format. — [99Food – Giraffas Botucatu (snippet)](https://99app.com/99food/botucatu/giraffas-botucatu/5764616012260182240/)

### Inferences
- [HYPOTHESIS] **PF/comercial:** a cozinheira-chefe runs production in batch for lunch. Salada de maionese appears on fixed days (e.g. weekend or a "dia da maionese") or as a side for frango/bife. Mayo is bought in balde with the general "rancho". **À la carte:** mayo appears in molhos (tártaro, rosé, aioli), with lower volume per dish but more technical requirements (emulsion stability when flavoured).

### Gaps
- No Sebrae, Abrasel or academic description of PF kitchen structure with mayo use was readable this round.

## Q6. Restaurante por quilo / self-service, churrascaria (cold buffet)

### Takeaway
Salada de maionese is a **classic buffet item** in both por quilo and churrascaria buffets, and Brazilian studies repeatedly flag it as a temperature and contamination hotspot (above 10 °C on display). Here mayo is consumed in bulk inside a preparation, and the dominant need is **stability and safety on display**.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT] Mayo salad is "uma das opções frequentemente servidas" in Brazilian restaurants. Commercial restaurants account for about half of bacterial outbreaks in Brazil, and **salada de maionese com batatas** is among the preparations most often involved (academic review). — [PUC-Campinas Revista de Nutrição (snippet)](https://seer.sis.puc-campinas.edu.br/nutricao/article/download/9395/6765/35530); [Journal Health Science – Salmonella e Listeria em saladas com maionese (snippet)](https://journalhealthscience.pgsskroton.com.br/article/download/3583/3301)
- [FACT] In Guanambi (BA), mayo salad reached the refrigerated counter above 10 °C. Another study found the salad at inadequate temperatures (above 10 °C) during distribution. In Belo Horizonte, 60% of self-service por quilo restaurants had non-conformities in distribution temperature. — [Demetra/UERJ (snippet)](https://www.e-publicacoes.uerj.br/demetra/article/download/15317/13284/58816); [UNIPAR via DOAJ (snippet)](https://doaj.org/article/0de9a544e67346bebc82b10e1dfcdde0)
- [FACT] An old São Paulo study (1982) noted the popular belief that mayo salads spoil quickly. Its data did not confirm this generally, but it found faecal coliforms in samples. — [SciELO Saúde Pública 1982 (snippet)](https://scielosp.org/pdf/rsp/1982.v16n6/307-316/pt)
- [FACT] Churrascaria: rodízio meat service plus a self-service buffet of salads, bread, rice and farofa. Salada de maionese (potato, carrot, mayo, vinegar, parsley) is described as "traditionally served with churrasco". — [Wikipedia – Churrascaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Churrascaria); [TasteAtlas – Porto Alegre (snippet)](https://www.tasteatlas.com/best-dishes-in-porto-alegre)

### Inferences
- [INTERPRETATION] **Differentiation:** the por quilo sells by weight with a hot + cold buffet, lunch peak, batch production and sometimes a nutricionista responsável. The churrascaria buffet is a complement to meat (rodízio or espeto corrido). In both, mayo is a **bulk ingredient** (kg per batch), and mayo salad volume is probably among the highest per establishment of any type.
- [HYPOTHESIS] Buyers favour large packs (balde 3 kg+ or galão). The criteria are yield when mixed, resistance to "soltar água"/separating when combined with cooked vegetables, and holding at buffet temperature. Some operators may use **mayo-type "molho"** products (cheaper, lower oil) for salads. Check labels in the field.
- [HYPOTHESIS] Prep sequence: cook vegetables in the morning → cool → mix → refrigerate → replenish the buffet in small batches (better practice) or put it all out at once (riskier). Observe it.

### Gaps
- No data on kg of mayo per day in por quilo restaurants. Abrasel content on self-service was not reachable.

## Q7. Pizzaria (salão vs. delivery)

### Takeaway
No evidence was found of mayo as a pizza topping or on the borda in SP or Recife (only Japan appeared in results). Mayo appears as a **paid side** (sachê R$ 0.99; 2 × 7 g sachês R$ 1.00; "Maionese D Casa" plain or temperada sold as a side in an RJ pizzaria). The Kraft Heinz/Toluna figure (20% eat pizza with mayo) from round 1 is the only consumption datum.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT] Sachê and side evidence: Pizzaria Inovação (Salvador) sachê de maionese R$ 0.99; Pizzaria Copacabana (BH) "Maionese Sachê 7 Gramas – 2 Unidades" R$ 1.00; Le Patron Pizzaria (RJ) sells "Maionese D Casa" plain and seasoned as a side. — [99Food – Inovação (snippet)](https://99app.com/99food/salvador/pizzaria-inovaao/5764608449229620463/); [99Food – Copacabana (snippet)](https://99app.com/99food/belo-horizonte/pizzaria-copacabana/5764608591622046898/); [99Food – Le Patron (snippet)](https://99app.com/99food/rio-de-janeiro/le-patron-pizzaria/5764610424797135567/)
- [FACT] Northeast context: Ceará leads Northeast pizza consumption (4.8 mi iFood pizzas in 2024). Calabresa is the most-ordered pizza in Pernambuco (iFood Tendências). — [Diário do Nordeste (snippet)](https://diariodonordeste.verdesmares.com.br/negocios/cearenses-consomem-em-media-24-pizzas-por-ano-e-negocios-faturam-r-12-bilhao-1.3671300); [Movimento Econômico – Dia da Pizza 2026 (snippet)](https://movimentoeconomico.com.br/economia/consumo/2026/07/10/dia-da-pizza-nordeste-ganha-espaco-no-mercado-nacional-de-delivery/)

### Inferences
- [INTERPRETATION] In pizzaria, mayo is a **delivery add-on** handled at the packing station (sachês in the box), not in the forno/bancada de montagem. The relevant pack is the **portion pack**, and the buying criterion is unit cost per sachê.
- [HYPOTHESIS] Some pizzarias that also sell lanches or hot dogs (common in neighbourhood delivery) use balde mayo for those items. Others may use mayo in "frango com catupiry"-style or "strogonoff" toppings. Neither was verified; ask in the field. "Maionese temperada for pizza" in some regions: **no evidence found**.

### Gaps
- Regional practice of mayo on pizza (e.g. in the interior or the Nordeste) is undocumented.

## Q8. QSR chains (McDonald's/Arcos Dorados, BK/Zamp, Bob's, Subway, Habib's, Giraffas) and regional chains

### Takeaway
Chains turn mayo into **branded menu assets and sellable SKUs**:
- Giraffas sells its "maionese caseira Giraffas" in a 350 g jar (R$ 26.90 on a listing).

## Q14. Cozinhas institucionais / refeições coletivas (industrial canteens, hospitals, schools)

### Takeaway
**School feeding (PNAE): mayonnaise is on the list of products that cannot be bought with program funds**, alongside refrigerantes, glutamato monossódico and powders for reconstitution.
